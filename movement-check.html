BALANCE

Stand on one leg

Stand on one leg with your eyes open. Stop the timer when the raised foot touches down. Use the best of 3 attempts.

Use a clear space and have something stable nearby to hold if needed.
02
LEG STRENGTH

5 × sit-to-stand

Start seated. Stand fully and sit down 5 times as quickly and safely as you can. Record the total time.

Use a firm chair. Do not perform the test if it is unsafe for you.

THE RESEARCH

Where the reference values come from

Single-leg stance

Springer et al. studied 549 healthy adults aged 18–99. Eyes-open performance decreased with age, and the study found performance was age-specific rather than gender-dependent.

View the study →
5 × sit-to-stand

A large pooled analysis of 45,470 adults aged 50+ from 14 European countries provides age- and sex-stratified reference percentiles for the five-times-sit-to-stand test.

View the research →

These are reference values from research populations, not a diagnosis or a pass/fail test. Test technique, footwear, chair height, health conditions and other factors can affect results. If you are concerned about balance, falls or mobility, speak with a physiotherapist or other health professional.
