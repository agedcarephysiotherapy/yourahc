WHO WE ARE

Allied health that understands real life.

Your Allied Health Care (Your AHC) is an adult allied health service from Aged Care Physiotherapy Pty Ltd.

We support adults across different stages of life with practical physiotherapy and occupational therapy focused on movement, independence, participation and everyday life.

Our clinicians work with people, families, carers, workplaces and support teams to turn assessment into practical action.

OUR SERVICES

Two disciplines. One practical approach.

From injury recovery and rehabilitation to disability support, independence and healthy ageing, we tailor care to the person and their goals.

01

Physiotherapy

Support movement and physical function for adults, from sports and other injuries to rehabilitation, chronic conditions, disability support and maintaining mobility as we age.

  • Movement, mobility and gait
  • Strength, balance and exercise
  • Injury and post-operative rehabilitation
  • Pain and physical function
  • Neurological and disability support
  • Falls prevention and safe transfers
Request Physiotherapy
02

Occupational Therapy

Practical support to help people build skills, participate in everyday activities and manage life at home, school, work and in the community.

  • Functional and capacity assessment
  • Daily living and independence
  • Home, school and workplace needs
  • Assistive technology and equipment
  • Participation and meaningful activities
  • Practical strategies for families and support teams
Request Occupational Therapy
WHY YOUR AHC

Good care should fit real life.

Good allied health should be clinically sound and useful outside the assessment room. We focus on what the person wants to do, what is getting in the way and what can realistically help.

01

Experienced clinical team

Your AHC is backed by the clinical experience and systems of Aged Care Physiotherapy Pty Ltd.

02

For every stage of adulthood

We adapt our approach to the person, whether they are a younger adult, working-age adult or older adult.

03

Practical recommendations

We aim to make recommendations that people, families and support teams can actually use.

04

People around you matter

With consent, we work with families, carers, schools, workplaces and support teams so care carries into everyday life.
