WHO WE ARE

Allied health that understands real life.

Your Allied Health Care (Your AHC) is Aged Care Physiotherapy trading as Your Allied Health Care.

We bring the clinical experience and practical approach of an established Melbourne physiotherapy provider into allied health services that are focused on function, independence, participation and everyday goals.

Our clinicians work with people, families, carers and support teams to turn assessment into practical action.

WHY YOUR AHC

Clinical experience, without making life complicated.

Good allied health should be clinically sound and useful outside the assessment room. We focus on what the person needs to do, what is getting in the way and what can realistically help.

01

02

Built around the person

03

Practical recommendations
