WHY YOUR AHC

Therapy should make everyday life easier.

At Your AHC, we look beyond a diagnosis or a single treatment goal. We consider the person, their environment, routines, strengths and the things they want to be able to do.

Our clinicians work alongside participants, families, support workers and other professionals to turn therapy into practical strategies that can be used in real life.

HOW WE WORK

Care that starts with what matters to you.

Good allied health care is more than a treatment session. We understand what the participant wants to achieve, assess function in context, develop practical strategies and keep the wider support team informed.

FOR SUPPORT COORDINATORS & REFERRERS

Simple referrals.
Clear communication.

Whether you are a support coordinator, plan manager, support worker, family member or another health professional, we aim to make referrals straightforward from the first conversation.

  • Prompt contact and initial discussion
  • Functional, goal-focused assessment
  • Clear recommendations and reports
  • Practical communication with the broader support team
  • Support that connects with everyday participation
