NDIS • PHYSIOTHERAPY • OCCUPATIONAL THERAPY

Getting the right therapy shouldn't be this hard.

You may know you need Physio or OT, but finding the right therapist, organising appointments and making therapy work in everyday life can be difficult.

WE UNDERSTAND

It's not just about finding a therapist.

Good therapy needs to fit the person, their goals, their routine and the people around them. Here are some of the things participants and families can struggle with, and how we try to make each one easier.

01

“I don't know where to start.”

The NDIS can be confusing, especially when you are accessing therapy for the first time.

How we help

We listen first. We discuss what you are trying to achieve and whether Physio or OT may be appropriate for your needs and goals.

02

“I can't find the right therapist.”

You may need someone who understands your disability, communication style, physical needs or everyday challenges.

How we help

We take time to understand you as a person and adapt our approach to your needs rather than working from a diagnosis alone.

03

“It's hard to get an appointment.”

Availability, travel and finding a service that fits around your life can all be barriers.

How we help

Where appropriate, we provide therapy in the home or community and work with you to find practical appointment arrangements.

04

“I don't understand my NDIS funding.”

You may not know whether therapy is funded, how it relates to your goals or what your plan allows.

How we help

We explain the therapy and funding pathway in straightforward language and work within your available funding and plan arrangements.

05

“Nothing changes between appointments.”

A session is only one part of therapy. Recommendations can be difficult to carry over into everyday life.

How we help

We make therapy practical, with strategies and recommendations that can be used at home, in the community and with your support team where appropriate.

06

“I keep having to explain everything again.”

Changing therapists or providers can mean repeatedly going through your history and needs.

How we help

We aim for continuity and clear communication, keeping your goals and progress at the centre of the therapy.

07

“My support team isn't on the same page.”

Physios, OTs, support workers, family and other professionals may all be involved.

How we help

With your consent, we can communicate with the people supporting you and make recommendations clear and practical.

08

“I need an assessment or report.”

Functional assessments, equipment assessments and reports can be difficult to understand or organise.

How we help

We assess what is relevant to your situation and explain what we are doing and why. Where appropriate, we provide clear functional recommendations.

09

“I don't want therapy just for the sake of therapy.”

You want therapy to make a meaningful difference to your everyday life.

How we help

We focus on practical outcomes such as moving more safely, managing pain, building strength, using equipment, getting out into the community or becoming more independent with daily activities.

THE WAY WE WORK

Therapy should fit real life.

We don't expect you to have everything figured out before contacting us. Tell us what's difficult. We'll help you work out the next step.
