SUPPORT AT HOME

How the program works.

Support at Home replaced the Home Care Packages Program and Short-term Restorative Care Programme in November 2025. If you are approved, your funding and approved services are based on your assessed needs.

01 • CLINICAL SUPPORTS

Physiotherapy & Occupational Therapy

Support at Home includes clinical supports such as occupational therapy and physiotherapy. These services can help restore movement and function, improve safety, support independence and help you remain at home.

Clinical support services such as physiotherapy are fully funded by the Australian Government under the current contribution arrangements.

02 • RESTORATIVE CARE

Regain function & independence

The Restorative Care Pathway provides short-term, coordinated allied health and/or nursing services to help you regain function and stay independent.

It is designed to support meaningful everyday activities and may help delay the need for more intensive ongoing services.

03 • AT & HOME MODIFICATIONS

Equipment & safer homes

The Assistive Technology and Home Modifications scheme provides separate upfront funding for assessed equipment and home modifications.

Occupational therapy may help assess functional needs and recommend appropriate equipment or changes to the home.

HOW IT CAN WORK

Assessment → approval → your services.

You choose the provider and work with them to decide the mix of approved services that suits your needs and budget.

01

Get assessed

An aged care assessment determines whether you are eligible for Support at Home and what level of support you need.

02

Receive your funding

If approved, you are assigned an ongoing classification or a short-term pathway based on your assessed needs.

03

Choose your provider

You can choose a service provider. You and your provider discuss the mix of approved services that fits your needs and budget.

04

Review as needs change

You can ask to change your service mix or request a reassessment if your needs change.

Good to know: You do not make a contribution towards clinical support services such as physiotherapy. Contributions for other service types can depend on the type of service and your financial circumstances.
