Get started
01 • START HERE

Therapy starts with what matters to you.

Physiotherapy and occupational therapy look at the person, not just the condition, and turn goals into practical steps.

Physiotherapy

Movement, strength, balance, pain, mobility and physical function.

Occupational Therapy

Daily activities, independence, equipment, home and community participation.

02 • STROKE

After a stroke, movement can be rebuilt.

Rehabilitation can focus on walking, transfers, balance, arm and hand function and getting back to everyday activities.

Physiotherapy

Gait, balance, strength, transfers and physical conditioning.

Occupational Therapy

Self-care, upper-limb function, cognition, equipment and daily routines.

03 • PARKINSON'S DISEASE

Make movement safer and easier.

Therapy can help people manage changes in walking, balance, transfers and everyday tasks.

Physiotherapy

Walking practice, balance, strength, mobility and exercise.

Occupational Therapy

Strategies for dressing, showering, home safety, routines and equipment.

04 • MULTIPLE SCLEROSIS

Manage fatigue while staying active.

A tailored approach can balance movement, energy, safety and participation in everyday life.

Physiotherapy

Strength, balance, mobility, fitness and fatigue-aware exercise.

Occupational Therapy

Energy conservation, routines, equipment and practical task strategies.

05 • ACQUIRED BRAIN INJURY

Build function into everyday life.

Rehabilitation can address physical, cognitive and functional challenges after an acquired brain injury.

Physiotherapy

Mobility, balance, coordination, strength and safe transfers.

Occupational Therapy

Daily living, routines, cognition, equipment and community participation.

06 • CEREBRAL PALSY

Support movement, comfort and independence.

Therapy can be adapted across adulthood to support physical function and participation.

Physiotherapy

Strength, mobility, gait, balance, positioning and conditioning.

Occupational Therapy

Equipment, seating, self-care, home access and everyday independence.

07 • SPINAL CORD INJURY

Work towards safer independence.

A coordinated approach can address mobility, transfers, positioning and everyday participation.

Physiotherapy

Transfers, strength, wheelchair mobility, conditioning and movement.

Occupational Therapy

Seating, equipment, personal care, home access and daily tasks.

08 • AMPUTATION & LIMB LOSS

Move with confidence and purpose.

Rehabilitation can help with mobility, balance, strength and adapting to everyday activities.

Physiotherapy

Gait, balance, strengthening, conditioning and prosthetic rehabilitation.

Occupational Therapy

Daily tasks, equipment, home setup and adapting activities.

09 • CHRONIC PAIN & ARTHRITIS

Keep doing more of what matters.

Therapy can help people build capacity and adapt activities around pain and function.

Physiotherapy

Exercise, mobility, strength, pacing and movement strategies.

Occupational Therapy

Task modification, routines, equipment and energy-saving strategies.

10 • FALLS & LOSS OF FUNCTION

Stay safer at home and in the community.

Physiotherapy and OT can work together to identify practical ways to improve safety and independence.

Physiotherapy

Strength, balance, gait, transfers and mobility confidence.

Occupational Therapy

Home safety, equipment, routines and everyday task strategies.
