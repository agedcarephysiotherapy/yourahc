PHYSIOTHERAPY • ADULTS • NDIS
STROKE

Physiotherapy for Stroke

A stroke can affect strength, balance, coordination, walking, transfers and the ability to use one side of the body. Physiotherapy can support rehabilitation and help maintain physical function.

Request Physiotherapy →
CONDITION

Practical support for everyday function.

Physiotherapy is tailored to the person's abilities, environment and goals. Your physiotherapist will assess current function and develop a program around what is important to you.

PROBLEMS YOU MAY FACE
  • Weakness on one side of the body
  • Difficulty walking or changing direction
  • Poor balance and coordination
  • Difficulty standing or transferring
  • Reduced endurance
  • Falls or fear of falling
  • Difficulty using mobility aids effectively
GOALS
  • Improve walking safety and distance
  • Improve balance and coordination
  • Increase strength
  • Improve standing tolerance
  • Make transfers more independent
  • Reduce falls risk
  • Increase confidence with community mobility
PHYSIOTHERAPY INTERVENTIONS
  • Gait retraining and repetitive task practice
  • Balance and coordination training
  • Strengthening
  • Transfer practice
  • Functional standing and mobility exercises
  • Falls prevention
  • Walking aid assessment and training
YOUR GOALS COME FIRST

Care built around what matters to you.

Depending on your circumstances, physiotherapy may support NDIS goals relating to mobility, independence, participation, safety, exercise and functional capacity. Outcomes vary between individuals and goals are reviewed as needs change.
