Physiotherapy for Motor Neurone Disease

Motor neurone disease can progressively affect muscle strength and movement. Physiotherapy can help manage physical changes, maintain function where possible and develop safe strategies as needs change.

Request Physiotherapy →
HOW PHYSIOTHERAPY CAN HELP

Practical support for everyday function.

Physiotherapy is tailored to the person's abilities, environment and goals. Your physiotherapist will assess current function and develop a program around what is important to you.

PHYSIOTHERAPY MAY INCLUDE
  • Maintaining movement and flexibility
  • Gentle strengthening where appropriate
  • Mobility and walking
  • Transfers
  • Falls and injury prevention
  • Positioning
  • Equipment and mobility aids
  • Pain and discomfort management
  • Energy conservation
  • Fatigue management
  • Respiratory physiotherapy where within scope and appropriate
  • Carer and support worker education
GOALS WE MAY WORK TOWARDS
  • Maintaining mobility for as long as possible
  • Making transfers safer
  • Maintaining joint movement
  • Reducing discomfort associated with positioning or movement
  • Conserving energy during daily activities
  • Using equipment safely
  • Reducing falls and injury risk
  • Supporting independence where possible
  • Helping carers and support workers provide safe physical assistance
  • Maintaining participation in meaningful activities
YOUR GOALS COME FIRST

Care built around what matters to you.

Depending on your circumstances, physiotherapy may support NDIS goals relating to mobility, independence, participation, safety, exercise and functional capacity. Outcomes vary between individuals and goals are reviewed as needs change.
