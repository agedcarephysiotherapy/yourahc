FUNDING • NDIS

How the NDIS can fund allied health.

The NDIS can fund disability-related supports that help participants build skills, maintain or improve independence and participate in everyday life. Physiotherapy and occupational therapy may be funded when they meet NDIS funding requirements.

NDIS FUNDING

Funding is based on your needs and goals.

NDIS plans are individual. The supports available to you depend on your disability-related needs, goals, evidence and the funding included in your plan. YOUR can help you understand where physiotherapy or occupational therapy may fit within your approved supports.

01 • THERAPY

Physiotherapy & Occupational Therapy

NDIS therapy supports can help improve skills and independence with everyday activities, including mobility and movement, personal care, functioning and community living.

Examples of funded therapy supports include physiotherapy and occupational therapy, where the support meets the NDIS funding criteria.

02 • CAPACITY BUILDING

Improved Daily Living

Improved Daily Living is a Capacity Building support category. It can fund supports that build living skills and increase independence and participation in the community, including assessments, training and therapies.

NDIS guidance gives occupational therapy as an example and states that therapy funding in a Capacity Building budget may be used for therapy supports such as physiotherapy or occupational therapy, according to the stated support in the plan.

03 • FUNCTION

Building independence in real life

Therapy can focus on practical outcomes such as walking and mobility, transfers, personal care, communication, daily routines, community access and other activities affected by disability.

An assessment may also provide evidence about functional capacity and recommendations for therapies, assistive technology or home modifications.

HOW IT CAN WORK

From your goals to practical support.

The exact pathway depends on your NDIS plan and how your funding is managed.

01

Identify your goals

Think about what you want to do more safely, independently or confidently in everyday life.

02

Understand your plan

Check the relevant support category and whether the therapy support is stated in your plan.

03

Complete assessment

Your physiotherapist or OT can assess function, identify disability-related barriers and provide recommendations or evidence where appropriate.

04

Work towards outcomes

Therapy can then focus on practical skills, independence, mobility, participation and the goals that matter to you.

Important: NDIS funding is not automatic. The NDIS must determine that a support meets its funding criteria, and some supports are stated and cannot be swapped for other supports. Your plan and circumstances determine what can be purchased.
