Physiotherapy for Multiple Sclerosis

Multiple sclerosis can affect strength, balance, coordination, walking, endurance and fatigue. Physiotherapy can help people maintain physical capacity, manage movement difficulties and continue activities that are important to them.

Request Physiotherapy →
CONDITION

Practical support for everyday function.

Physiotherapy is tailored to the person's abilities, environment and goals. Your physiotherapist will assess current function and develop a program around what is important to you.

PROBLEMS YOU MAY FACE
  • Fatigue affecting daily activities
  • Reduced strength or endurance
  • Balance difficulties
  • Changes in walking ability
  • Muscle stiffness or reduced flexibility
  • Falls or reduced confidence
  • Difficulty managing activity levels
GOALS
  • Maintain or improve strength
  • Improve balance and walking safety
  • Manage fatigue more effectively
  • Maintain flexibility
  • Improve endurance within individual tolerance
  • Reduce falls risk
  • Maintain independence and participation
PHYSIOTHERAPY INTERVENTIONS
  • Individualised strengthening
  • Balance and gait training
  • Fatigue and activity management
  • Flexibility and mobility exercises
  • Endurance training within tolerance
  • Falls prevention
  • Mobility aid and exercise advice
YOUR GOALS COME FIRST

Care built around what matters to you.

Depending on your circumstances, physiotherapy may support NDIS goals relating to mobility, independence, participation, safety, exercise and functional capacity. Outcomes vary between individuals and goals are reviewed as needs change.
